**Subject:** Ultrasound BCI just got a factory: Gestala's $62M — plus at-home tDCS lands in US clinics

## The Signal

Gestala closed an RMB 420M (~$62M) Angel+ round on July 3 — RMB 570M raised within six months of a January launch — led by Huaying Capital with C Capital, HongShan, Lens Technology, and Sinovation among the backers. The money is going somewhere unusual for a BCI company this young: a 16,000 m² Chengdu headquarters built for Class II/III active-device development *and production*, paired with a Shanghai hub for brain foundation models. Technology: focused-ultrasound neuromodulation combined with functional ultrasound imaging — a non-invasive read-*and*-write play, entering through chronic pain.

Why it matters: while US BCI capital concentrates on implants, China's ultrasound path just got industrial-scale manufacturing before most Western competitors have a pivotal trial. If transcranial ultrasound holds up clinically, the cost curve — no surgery, factory-scale hardware — undercuts implant economics from below. Watch who follows with ultrasound filings in the next two quarters. [editorial — source: NeuroFounders, Jul 2026]

## Regulatory

**Flow Neuroscience FL-100** — the first at-home prescription neuromodulation therapy authorized in the US for moderate-to-severe major depressive disorder: tDCS to prefrontal cortex, cleared off a Breakthrough Device Designation (2022) and a 174-participant sham-controlled RCT published in *Nature Medicine*; US availability began Q2 2026. Strategic read: home neuromodulation for psychiatry is now a prescribable category — clinic-based TMS providers must decide whether home devices are channel or competitor. [Patient Care Online]

**Naox Technologies "Naox Link"** — first FDA-cleared in-ear EEG (510(k), Jan 6 2026): extended real-world brain monitoring, overnight wear, no gels. Strategic read: ambulatory EEG's form-factor barrier just fell; expect epilepsy-monitoring and sleep companies to license or copy the ear canal as the electrode site. [Pharmacy Times]
