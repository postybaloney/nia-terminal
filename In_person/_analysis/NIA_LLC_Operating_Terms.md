# Neurotech Intelligence Agency, LLC — Operating Terms

*Decisions from the founder conversation (Aug 2026), written up so they can be formalized. This is a plain-language term sheet to hand an attorney or use as the basis of the operating agreement — not legal advice. One attorney-hour to review before signing is money well spent.*

## 1. Entity
- **Form:** Member-managed **LLC**. No outside capital raise or aggressive expansion planned — so no stock, option pool, or investor share class is needed; membership interests (percentages) are the whole cap table.
- **State:** form in one state (SC, where Parth is, is cheapest/simplest; Delaware only if you later expect investors). Members can live in different states — fine for an LLC.
- **Name / brand:** Neurotech Intelligence Agency (spelled out), neurotechintelligenceagency.com.

## 2. Members & membership interests
| Member | Interest | Primary role |
|---|---|---|
| Daniel Kim | **33%** | Editorial / growth / the Neurotech Napkin |
| Luc LaMontagne | **28% or 24%** | Clinical & regulatory analysis, industry distribution |
| Parth Desai | **24% or 28%** | Data engineering — pipeline + Terminal |

- Daniel is the lead founder at 33%. Luc and Parth take **28% and 24%** — decide between yourselves which is which (a coin-flip or a quick "who's carrying more this quarter" call; write it down once chosen).
- **The remaining 15% is a reserved contributor/advisor pool (DECIDED).** 33 + 28 + 24 = 85% to the founders; **15% held unallocated** for future contributors and advisors, granted by majority vote. Until granted it stays unissued — the founders' 85% carries 100% of the votes and profit share pro-rata, so nothing is "owned by no one." Your earlier 5/5/5 advisor sketch fits inside this pool.

## 3. Management & decisions
- **Member-managed**, majority by interest for ordinary decisions (pricing, content, tools, spend under a set cap).
- **Unanimous** for major actions: admitting a new member, selling/dissolving, taking on debt or investment, or anything that changes the membership split.

## 4. Intellectual property
- **All work product created for the venture is assigned to the LLC** on creation (code written for NIA, content, brand, subscriber data).
- **Parth licenses the pre-existing pipeline + Terminal to the LLC (DECIDED):** the license is **exclusive to the LLC within the neurotech-industry-intelligence field, perpetual, royalty-free, and survives Parth's departure** — so the product is never hostage to one member. Parth retains ownership of the underlying technology and may reuse it outside this field (other verticals, Voyage, etc.).

## 5. Contributions, costs & money
- **Infra costs** (hosting, API keys, domain, email) are paid from revenue first; **until there's revenue, split evenly**, capped (e.g., ≤$50/member/month) without unanimous consent.
- **Profits/losses allocated by membership %.** Distributions when the members vote to distribute (majority).
- **Banking:** open a dedicated NIA business account — **separate from Epsilon Solutions LLC** (that's Parth's solo entity; do not commingle).

## 6. Vesting (DECIDED)
- Membership interests vest on a **4-year schedule with a 1-year cliff**, backdated to each member's real start date, enforced as a repurchase/forfeiture right in the operating agreement. Leave before the cliff → forfeit your interest; after → keep the vested portion. Applies equally to all three.

## 7. Conflicts of interest (confirmed)
- **No work on employer time/equipment** — confirmed by all.
- **No member writes editorial/opinion coverage of their own employer** (Daniel/Somnee, Luc/Ampa, Parth/Voyage). The **deterministic pipeline data** (filings, clearances, grants, trials, funding) publishes neutrally regardless — that's the firewall that keeps coverage credible.
- Each member should confirm their own employment/IP agreement permits this side venture (standard moonlighting clauses — a 5-minute check).

## 8. Departure
- Unvested interest is subject to forfeiture (per §6). Vested interest is retained; the members may agree a future-revenue step-down for an inactive member. No clawback on past distributions.
