## The strategy (champion → share → seed)
The terminal sells itself in 30 seconds *if the right person opens it*. So the whole game is getting a few credible, well-networked people to look and pass it on:

1. **Champions first.** 3–5 people whose forward-to-a-friend actually carries weight (Naveen is the marquee one). Ask for a reaction + one intro each. Warm intros out-convert any cold campaign you could run.
2. **Seed the venues.** Bakar Labs, Berkeley SkyDeck, QB3, neuro/BCI labs, and any biotech Slack/newsletter you can reach. These are rooms full of the exact user.
3. **Free early access, on purpose.** Give it away to the first cohort. You're buying usage + testimonials + word-of-mouth, not revenue yet. Every user who says "I check this weekly" is your next pitch's proof.
4. **The artifact is the link.** Don't attach decks. Send the live snapshot URL — it *is* the demo. Once you have 5–10 regular users and a couple of quotes, that's when a paid tier or a sponsor conversation becomes real.

Keep every send warm, specific, and to **one** low-friction ask. These are your people — write like it.
